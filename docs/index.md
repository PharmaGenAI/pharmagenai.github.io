<p class="section-intro">Start with the decision in front of you. Use one capability on its own, or connect outputs where the workflow and your governance allow it. The sequence below is a portfolio map—not an autonomous process.</p>

  <h2 id="beta-boundary">Public beta means review is part of the workflow</h2>
  <p>Validate identity, source coverage, assumptions, data permissions, and rendered artifacts before operational use. Campaign and field-training materials are drafts for qualified medical/legal/regulatory review—not evidence of approval. Recommendations do not replace accountable business, medical, legal, privacy, or regulatory judgement.</p>
