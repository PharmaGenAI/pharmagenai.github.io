# Start with the business outcome

Open Pharma Plugins is a set of independently installable capabilities. Choose the narrowest workflow that
supports the decision at hand, then connect artifacts only when their identifiers, provenance, and governance
remain intact.

<div class="preview-dossier" markdown="1">
  <div class="preview-label">Outcome 01<br>Know the account</div>
  <div markdown="1">
## Prepare for an evidence-informed HCP or HCO conversation

**Use HCP Intelligence** to assemble a structured profile from public sources such as ORCID, PubMed,
ClinicalTrials.gov, and selected web providers. The useful outcome is not a score: it is a reviewable account
brief where claims carry sources and confidence.

- **Bring:** an identified HCP or HCO, plus affiliation, specialty, or country to help disambiguate.
- **Review:** identity match, source authority, incomplete coverage, and recency.
- **Decision supported:** account preparation and research prioritisation—not automated targeting.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="preview-dossier" markdown="1">
  <div class="preview-label">Outcome 02<br>Read the market</div>
  <div markdown="1">
## Create one evidence base for a landscape review

**Use Competitive Intelligence** to collect bounded public-source evidence across trials, regulatory records,
publications, and configured news search. One immutable run can feed both a briefing and a timeline, reducing
the risk that separate views silently use different evidence.

- **Bring:** explicit drug or company identities and a defined monitoring question.
- **Review:** source status, query scope, retrieval time, record count, and stated limitations.
- **Decision supported:** landscape and launch-planning discussions—not a claim that no event occurred when coverage failed.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="preview-dossier" markdown="1">
  <div class="preview-label">Outcome 03<br>Shape coverage</div>
  <div markdown="1">
## Compare territory choices before committing the field

**Use Territory Alignment** to model assignments, vacancies, new hires, overrides, and visit clusters while
showing trade-offs among workload, travel, relationship continuity, and priority coverage.

- **Bring:** governed HCP, representative, territory, and location data.
- **Review:** unassigned accounts, manager overrides, employment rules, map-data exposure, and operational feasibility.
- **Decision supported:** scenario comparison and route planning—not an automatic reorganisation.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="preview-dossier" markdown="1">
  <div class="preview-label">Outcome 04<br>Plan engagement</div>
  <div markdown="1">
## Turn a governed universe into a constrained engagement plan

**Use Next-Best-Engagement** to recommend an HCP, channel, and assigned representative while applying explicit
consent, capacity, minimum-gap, channel-diversity, and coverage rules.

- **Bring:** an assigned HCP universe with consent and recent-interaction data appropriate to the use case.
- **Review:** eligibility, unassigned reasons, channel permissions, stale inputs, and field practicality.
- **Decision supported:** call-plan review and what-if comparison—not autonomous outreach.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="preview-dossier" markdown="1">
  <div class="preview-label">Outcome 05<br>Equip the field</div>
  <div markdown="1">
## Build source-grounded learning and practice material

**Use Field Training** to turn supplied, approved PDF or PPTX documents into learning packages, assessments,
role-play kits, or scorecards. Each generated claim and model answer is expected to retain a source reference.

- **Bring:** the exact approved documents for this request.
- **Review:** source-set isolation, verbatim excerpts, fair balance, unsupported or off-label content, and rendering.
- **Decision supported:** training development and coaching—not permission for field use.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="preview-dossier" markdown="1">
  <div class="preview-label">Outcome 06<br>Prepare materials</div>
  <div markdown="1">
## Package campaign drafts for qualified MLR review

**Use Campaign Studio** to structure a brief, audience journey, message architecture, channel copy, validation
results, rendered assets, and a review package against a supplied approved-claims set and brand kit.

- **Bring:** jurisdiction, indication, audience, objective, approved claims, brand components, and required safety content.
- **Review:** claim support, fair balance, jurisdictional elements, asset fidelity, and current-input validation.
- **Decision supported:** campaign development and review preparation—not regulatory approval or distribution.
  </div>
</div>

## Where capabilities connect

Connections are optional and should be explicit. Territory assignments can become the owned universe for an
engagement plan. Source-grounded field material and claim-grounded campaign material can support a coordinated
enablement cycle. Competitive and HCP evidence can inform discussion, but it does not become approved promotional
content by crossing into another tool.

<aside class="evidence-note evidence-note--boundary" aria-labelledby="decision-boundary">
  <h2 id="decision-boundary">The accountable decision remains human</h2>
  <p>These workflows expose evidence, constraints, and artifacts for review. Teams remain responsible for data rights, privacy, identity, policy, medical accuracy, legal and regulatory review, approvals, and operational decisions. Public beta outputs must be validated before use.</p>
